Glyph — Comic Library
Designed for GNOME

Built for pure reading bliss

Every pixel is designed to feel right at home on modern Linux desktops with zero clutter.

Native Libadwaita

Crafted with GTK4 and Libadwaita to integrate seamlessly with your system theme, accent colors, and desktop animations.

Manga RTL Reading

Authentic right-to-left page turning designed specifically for Japanese manga, alongside standard western LTR modes.

Universal Format Support

Opens CBZ, CBR, PDF, ZIP, RAR, Tar, and 7z archives with blazing-fast unpacking and zero waiting.

Dual-Page Spreads

Enjoy two-page side-by-side spreads just like reading a physical comic book, with intelligent cover page handling.

100% Private & Offline

No trackers, no telemetry, no cloud sync required. Your reading habits and comic collection stay entirely on your machine.

Immersive Fullscreen

Tap F11 or double-click to hide all window chrome for a pure, distraction-free reading canvas with smooth zoom and pan.

Workflow

How Glyph Works

Get into your favorite stories in three straightforward steps.

1

Add Your Comics

Open individual comic archives or organize your entire collection folder into a clean visual bookshelf.

2

Pick Your Layout

Select single page, double-page spread, or toggle manga RTL flow with a single click or keyboard shortcut.

3

Read in Full Flow

Navigate smoothly using arrow keys, spacebar, or touch gestures. Your progress is saved automatically.
